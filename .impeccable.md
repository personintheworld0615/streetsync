# CivicPulse Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
- **The Commuter / Transit User**: Needs a fast, low-interaction interface to report road hazards and transit issues while moving. They rely on voice-activated reporting.
- **The Neighborhood Advocate**: Active citizens, pedestrians, and cyclists who report localized issues (e.g., ADA accessibility issues, illegal dumping). They want a tactile, map-centric, highly visual reporting tool.
- **Municipal Administrators**: City government staff who manage, triage, and resolve incoming tickets using the dashboard.

### Brand Personality
- **Friendly & Community-First**: Built for neighborhoods and real people. Empathy and warmth are key.
- **Fast & Responsive**: Every interaction removes barriers. Focus on instant response and voice/telemetry ease.
- **Trust-Based & Comforting**: Designed to evoke confidence that the city is listening and working.
- **Three-Word Brand Voice**: **Friendly, Community, Fast**

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Tone**: Warm Minimalist / Clean Public Utility. A bright, approachable, highly tactile look using soft borders and human touch elements.
- **Palette**: Clean white (`#FFFFFF`) base, warm soft whites/off-white (`oklch(0.98 0.003 70)`) for surfaces, comforting warm blue (`oklch(0.55 0.14 245)`) as primary accent, soft green (`oklch(0.65 0.14 140)`) for resolved items, and clean charcoal/dark slate (`oklch(0.25 0.01 240)`) for high-contrast readable typography. Avoid dark mode and heavy gradients.
- **Typography**: Display: *Space Grotesk* (clean, structured, and modern). Body: *Geist* or high-readability sans-serif.
- **Theme**: Pure light mode, warm and clean.

### Design Principles
1. **Bright, Accessible Tactility**: Keep layout cards flat or with very subtle borders. Use ample white space to let copy breathe.
2. **Human-Centric Simulator**: Focus simulator components on showing a friendly, easy-to-use phone screen mockup where residents report issues.
3. **Comforting Data Visualization**: Show reporting pipelines and government triage workflows in a simplified, transparent, and approachable way.
