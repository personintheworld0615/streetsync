# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
* **Residents & Commuters:** Looking for a frictionless way to report local issues (e.g., potholes, traffic hazards) safely and immediately.
* **Neighborhood Advocates:** Pedestrians, cyclists, and mobility-device users reporting local access barriers (e.g., blocked ramps, broken sidewalks).
* **Municipal Governments:** Administrators who need deduplicated, high-signal, severity-prioritized civic service requests.

## Product Purpose
StreetSync bridges the gap between citizens and city hall by removing the friction of reporting. The landing page serves as the entry point to build trust, showcase the app's capability, and drive engagement (app downloads for citizens and partnership inquiries for municipalities).

## Brand Personality
* **Three-word personality:** Trustworthy, Editorial, Restrained
* **Voice & Tone:** Professional and authoritative yet civic-minded and human. It must look like modern, high-quality public infrastructure software, not a flashy consumer-app startup.
* **Emotional Goals:** Evokes civic pride, confidence, and trust in the system.

## Anti-references
* **Municipal Portals:** Complex, cluttered, and hard-to-navigate legacy city web pages.
* **Generic SaaS Monoculture:** Heavy glassmorphism, glowing grids, bright neon purple/blue gradients, and generic card lists.
* **Over-rounded, playful layouts:** Avoid large `border-radius` (max 12–16px), diagonal background stripes, and cartoonish vector illustrations.

## Design Principles
1. **Command Civic Trust** — Establish authority through premium, editorial typography and crisp, intentional grids.
2. **Impact through Restraint** — Rely on empty space, structured layouts, and typographic scale rather than decorative shadows, gradients, or animations.
3. **Information First** — Present app features (Voice Reporting, Walking Mode, Municipal Pipeline) as a clean, structured hierarchy of information that is immediately readable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
* **WCAG 2.1 AA Compliance:** Minimum 4.5:1 contrast for body copy and interactive states.
* **Visual Adaptability:** Layout structures that remain readable when enlarged. Clean focus indicators and semantic hierarchy.
